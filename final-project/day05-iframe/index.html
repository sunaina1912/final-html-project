Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire is a 2023 Indian Telugu-language epic action thriller film written and directed by Prashanth Neel. Set in the fictional, dystopian city-state of Khansaar, the story centers on the intense friendship between Deva (played by Prabhas), an exiled prince, and Varadharaja "Vardha" Mannar (played by Prithviraj Sukumaran), the current heir to the throne. The narrative explores a high-stakes power struggle as Vardha enlists Deva's help to combat a coup d'état orchestrated by his father’s ministers and treacherous relatives. Produced by Hombale Films, the movie features an ensemble cast including Shruti Haasan, Jagapathi Babu, and Sriya Reddy, with music composed by Ravi Basrur. Upon its release on December 22, 2023, it became a major commercial success, grossing over ₹618 crore worldwide and ranking as one of the highest-grossing Telugu films of all time. A sequel titled Salaar: Part 2 – Shouryanga Parvam is currently in development for a planned 2026 release.

Hi Nanna

Hi Nanna (2023) is a poignant Telugu drama that follows Viraj (Nani), a single father and fashion photographer whose life centers around his six-year-old daughter, Mahi (Kiara Khanna), who suffers from cystic fibrosis. The narrative unfolds as Mahi’s persistent curiosity about her missing mother leads to a chance meeting with a woman named Yashna (Mrunal Thakur), whose arrival triggers a series of emotional revelations about Viraj’s past and his marriage to Varsha. Unlike the intense, violent father-son dynamics explored in the film Animal, which was released around the same time in December 2023, Hi Nanna was widely praised for its gentle, "therapeutic" exploration of parenthood and sensitive storytelling.
